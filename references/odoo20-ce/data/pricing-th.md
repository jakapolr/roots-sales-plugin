# Odoo subscription pricing & user types (th_TH)

> **`[web]` — NOT code-verified. As-of 2026-10-04.** Source: `odoo.com/th_TH/pricing`.
> **List price is quoted in USD** even on the Thai page (Odoo prices globally in USD). The th_TH page offers a **THB display** via the currency switcher (Odoo currency id 40 = ฿), but that is a **live FX conversion of the USD price, not a fixed THB list price** — always confirm the THB amount on the page / in a quote at the time of sale. Prices change; re-check before quoting.
> This is **Odoo's own subscription (Odoo Online / Enterprise licensing)** — separate from Roots/BEECY pricing and from implementation mandays.

## Plans (per user / per month, USD)

| Plan | Price (USD/user/mo) | Apps | Hosting | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One App Free** | **$0** | 1 app only | Odoo Online | unlimited users, 1 app |
| **Light User** | **$5.90** | restricted (see below) | Odoo Online | NEW user type — not a full user; quote via Odoo consultant |
| **Standard** | **$16.90** yearly · **$21.10** monthly | all apps | Odoo Online only | full internal user |
| **Custom** | **$32.00** yearly · **$40.00** monthly | all apps | Odoo Online · **Odoo.sh / On-premise** | adds **Odoo Studio, multi-company, external API, Agentic AI** |

- "Enterprise user" in current Odoo naming = a **full Internal User** on the **Standard** or **Custom** plan (Odoo retired the "Enterprise" plan label; it is now Standard / Custom). Full users pay the full per-user price above.
- Yearly billing is cheaper than monthly (both shown above). Discount noted as valid 12 months for initial users; hosting on Odoo.sh is extra.

## Light User (NEW) — what it is and can do

A **Light User** is an activated account flagged "Light User" in the system, or an active HR employee record not linked to a regular user. It is a cheaper, restricted user type (**$5.90/user/mo**) for staff who only need limited self-service or operational screens.

**As a user:** My Time Off · My Expenses · My Appraisals · My Attendances · My Referrals · My Planning · Field Service · Inventory barcode scanning · read-only Knowledge.
**As an employee:** receive payslips · use Point of Sale · use the Shop Floor control screen.

**How to get it:** not self-serve — book a call with an Odoo consultant for a tailored quote.

## Roots / BEECY note
- Odoo list price ≠ what Roots charges. For a client, model Odoo subscription (USD, per user/mo, full vs Light users) **separately** from Roots implementation (mandays, THB) and from BEECY SaaS.
- A deployment can mix **full users** (Standard/Custom) + **Light Users** ($5.90) + **POS/Shop-floor staff** to lower per-seat cost — useful in manufacturing/retail where many staff only need POS or shop-floor/HR access.
- For edition/hosting/BEECY definitions see `odoo-editions`; for the CE-vs-EE app boundary see `editions-ce-vs-ee.md`.
